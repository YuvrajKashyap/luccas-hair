# Decision 0003: Product Blueprint And MVP Scope

## Status

Superseded in part.

Later update: Book, About, and Contact were removed as standalone public pages. The current MVP scope is Home, Services, Products, and FAQ / Policies, with booking handled through the primary external Square CTA.

## Decision

Phase 2 compresses product planning into one complete blueprint instead of splitting sitemap, user flows, content planning, mockup planning, and technical direction into separate small phases.

The MVP scope is:

- Home.
- Services.
- Products.
- FAQ / Policies.

Booking is the primary conversion. Products are secondary and commerce details remain TBD until Lucca confirms product names, prices, photos, inventory, fulfillment, and platform choice.

Unknown client details stay marked as `TBD`. Implementation waits until mockups and assets are locked.

## Rationale

This keeps the project ready for mockups and implementation without rushing into a generic website build. The plan makes the business goals, user flows, content needs, integration choices, and technical guardrails clear before code is created.

## Impact

- The next phase can focus on mockups and asset lock.
- The booking path stays central across the site.
- Products are planned without pretending ecommerce is ready.
- Future implementation can follow approved page directions.
- The repository remains recruiter-friendly because planning decisions are visible and practical.
