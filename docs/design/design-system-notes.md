## Planned Color Palette Direction

- Deep charcoal or near-black base.
- Warm cream for text and surfaces.
- Brass or muted gold for accents.
- Soft neutral grays for borders and secondary text.
- Limited accent usage so the experience feels refined.

## Typography Direction

- Use a refined display style for major brand moments.
- Use a highly readable sans serif for navigation, body text, cards, and forms.
- Maintain strong contrast and mobile readability.
- Avoid novelty fonts that make the business feel less premium.

Exact font choices are TBD.

## Component Style Direction

- Appointment-first navigation and calls to action.
- Clear service cards or rows that are easy to scan.
- Product cards that can later support real images, prices, and detail links.
- Forms or embedded booking areas that feel clean and direct.
- Buttons should feel premium and tactile without being overly decorative.
- Cards should be restrained and purposeful.

## Motion Direction

- Motion should be subtle and polished.
- Use motion to support page transitions, reveal content, and guide attention.
- Avoid distracting animation around booking steps.
- Respect performance and accessibility.

## Spacing And Layout Direction

- Use generous spacing for premium feel.
- Keep booking actions visible and easy to reach.
- Design mobile layouts first for key conversion paths.
- Use strong page sections with clear hierarchy.
- Avoid cluttered layouts that bury services or booking.

## Image And Asset Direction

- Prefer real client-approved photography when available.
- Use generated or sourced imagery only when it supports the brand and is clearly appropriate.
- Keep all references, mockups, logo exports, and product images organized in `assets/`.
- Do not use final product images or business photos until approved.
