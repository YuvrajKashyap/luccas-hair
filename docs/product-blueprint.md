# Product Blueprint

## Product Summary

Lucca's Hair is an appointment-first website for a premium salon and barber-style brand. The MVP should help visitors understand the brand, review services, book an appointment, browse product offerings, and contact the business.

The project is currently in planning. No website implementation has started yet.

## Business Objective

Make appointment booking easy, obvious, and trustworthy for new and returning clients.

## Target Users

- New local visitors evaluating Lucca's style, services, and professionalism.
- Returning clients who want to book again quickly.
- Visitors comparing services before choosing an appointment.
- Product-interested clients who want to browse future hair or grooming items.
- Recruiters or portfolio reviewers evaluating planning, product thinking, UX, and implementation readiness.

## MVP Scope

- Home page with clear booking entry points and premium brand positioning.
- Services page with confirmed services, prices, and durations once available.
- Book page designed for an embedded booking module, external booking CTA, or direct contact path.
- Products page for a premium product catalog or showcase.
- About page for trust, background, and brand story once details are confirmed.
- Contact page with address, phone, email, hours, and social links once confirmed.
- FAQ / Policies page for booking, cancellation, product, and visit expectations once confirmed.

## Non-MVP Future Scope

- Product detail pages.
- Full ecommerce checkout.
- Gallery.
- Testimonials.
- Memberships.
- Gift cards.
- Blog or style journal.
- Client portal.
- Loyalty or email capture flows.

## Primary Conversion Goal

The primary conversion is booking an appointment. Booking should be visible in the main navigation, repeated in relevant page sections, and available through a sticky or mobile-friendly CTA.

## Secondary Conversion Goal

The secondary conversion is product interest. The MVP should support browsing or inquiry while keeping ecommerce details marked as `TBD` until product names, pricing, photos, inventory, and fulfillment are confirmed.

## Recommended Sitemap

- Home
- Services
- Book
- Products
- About
- Contact
- FAQ / Policies

Future pages can include Product Detail, Gallery, Testimonials, Memberships, Gift Cards, Blog / Style Journal, and Client Portal.

## Recommended Navigation

- Primary links: Home, Services, Products, About, Contact.
- Primary CTA: Book Appointment.
- Mobile navigation should keep Book Appointment visible without requiring deep menu exploration.
- If the booking platform is external, the CTA should clearly indicate that the user is leaving the site or opening the booking tool.

## Recommended Footer

- Booking CTA.
- Services link.
- Products link.
- Contact details, all TBD until confirmed.
- Hours, TBD.
- Location, TBD.
- Social links, TBD.
- FAQ / Policies link.
- Copyright line using the confirmed business name.

## UX Priorities

- Make booking the easiest action on every core page.
- Keep the visual tone premium, dark, warm, and restrained.
- Make services and products easy to scan on mobile.
- Avoid fake service, pricing, policy, product, and location details.
- Use clear copy that feels like a premium local business, not a generic template.
- Plan for future commerce without forcing ecommerce into the MVP before details are ready.

## Conversion Priorities

- Persistent Book Appointment CTA across desktop and mobile.
- Services page should guide users toward booking after comparing options.
- Book page should reduce friction by explaining the current booking method, TBD.
- Contact page should support users who are not ready to book online.
- Product page should invite inquiry or future purchase without pretending ecommerce is live.

## Risks And Unknowns

- Booking platform is TBD.
- Services, prices, and durations are TBD.
- Product names, prices, photos, inventory, and fulfillment are TBD.
- Address, phone, email, hours, and social links are TBD.
- Policies are TBD.
- Final logo files and approved photography are TBD.
- Domain is TBD.
- Building before mockups are locked could create a generic template result.

- Booking platform and preferred booking behavior.
- Product list, pricing, photos, and fulfillment method.
- Business address, phone number, email, hours, and service area.

- Final logo assets, photography permissions, and visual references.
