# Project Brief

## Overview

Lucca's Hair is a premium salon and barber-style website project. The site will support a real small business presence with an appointment-first experience, polished service presentation, and a future path for product sales.

The project should feel warm, stylish, masculine, clean, and Italian-inspired. It should avoid generic barber template styling and present Lucca as a premium, approachable hair professional.

## Business Goals

- Make booking appointments simple and prominent.
- Present Lucca as trusted, skilled, and professional.
- Explain services clearly once details are confirmed.
- Prepare for future product browsing and sales.
- Support local business credibility through clear contact, FAQ, policy, and brand content.
- Create a production-quality project that reflects strong planning, UX, and frontend execution.

## Target Users

- Existing clients who want to book again quickly.
- New clients evaluating Lucca's services and style.
- Local visitors comparing premium hair professionals.
- Product shoppers interested in recommended grooming or hair care items.
- Technical and portfolio reviewers evaluating client-style product work.

## Primary User Flow: Book An Appointment

1. User lands on the home page or another core page.
2. User sees a clear appointment call to action.
3. User reviews services or goes directly to booking.
4. User selects or follows the booking method, TBD.
5. User receives next-step confirmation through the selected platform or contact flow.

## Secondary User Flow: Browse Products

1. User opens the products page.
2. User browses available product categories, TBD.
3. User reviews product details once product content is confirmed.
4. User asks about a product, saves the information, or uses the future purchase path.
5. Future ecommerce details remain TBD until Lucca confirms platform, pricing, inventory, and fulfillment.

## MVP Pages

- Home
- Services
- Book
- Products
- About
- Contact
- FAQ / Policies

## Success Criteria

- Booking is visible, fast, and easy from core pages.
- The site communicates premium trust without feeling cold or generic.
- Services are easy to scan on mobile and desktop.
- Product planning can support future sales without restructuring the site.
- Content and design decisions are documented well enough for handoff.
- Production app infrastructure may exist before mockups, but final high-fidelity UI implementation waits until mockups and assets are locked.
