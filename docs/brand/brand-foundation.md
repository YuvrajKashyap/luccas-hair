## Brand Name

Lucca's Hair.

## Logo Direction

The logo direction is an illustrated barber or salon mascot mark. The earlier concept should be updated so the Lucca's Hair version uses an `L` instead of the earlier `T` concept.

## Tone

- Premium.
- Warm.
- Masculine.
- Stylish.
- Italian-inspired.
- Approachable.
- Clean.

## Visual Style

The visual style should feel cinematic, polished, and intimate. The brand can borrow from upscale barbershop and boutique salon cues without becoming a generic barber template.

The site should feel suitable for appointments, grooming, personal care, and trust.

## Color Direction

- Dark base colors for a cinematic foundation.
- Warm cream for contrast and readability.
- Tan, gold, or brass accents for premium details.
- Black accents and muted neutrals to keep the palette grounded.

Exact color values are TBD.

## Typography Direction

- Elegant display typography for brand moments.
- Clean, readable sans serif typography for interface and body copy.
- Type should feel refined, not flashy.
- Mobile readability must stay practical.

Exact typefaces are TBD.

## Voice

The voice should be confident, warm, and clear. It should sound like a premium local professional rather than a corporate chain.

Copy should emphasize ease of booking, craft, trust, and care. Service claims should stay factual and only use confirmed details.

## What The Brand Should Avoid

- Generic cheap barber-template energy.
- Overused clipper, pole, and beard graphics unless handled with restraint.
- Fake business details.
- Overly aggressive sales language.
- Cold luxury that feels unapproachable.
- Busy layouts that make booking harder.
- Stock-heavy visuals that do not feel connected to Lucca.
