# Lucca's Hair exact HQ service icon assets

This pack was built from the exact service icon strip supplied by Yuvraj.

Use for Codex:
- `transparent-png/*-256.png` or `transparent-png/*-512.png` for high-quality raster implementation.
- Display at about 40px in the service card.
- These PNGs are transparent, high-resolution, and preserve the approved mockup silhouette.
- `source-service-icon-strip.png` and `card-reference-crops/` are included so Codex can compare against the original.

Why PNG, not SVG only:
- The source is a raster mockup.
- The most accurate one-to-one result comes from preserving the original silhouette and upscaling it cleanly.
- Traced SVGs are included, but the PNGs are the recommended one-to-one assets.

Recommended files:
- mens-cuts: transparent-png/mens-cuts-256.png
- fades: transparent-png/fades-256.png
- beard-trim: transparent-png/beard-trim-256.png
- cut-and-beard: transparent-png/cut-and-beard-256.png
- grooming: transparent-png/grooming-256.png
- kids-seniors: transparent-png/kids-seniors-256.png
